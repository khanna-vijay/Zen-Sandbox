# 🙏 Gratitude & Bibliography

> *Nothing here is original. Improv is an oral tradition, passed hand to hand for seventy years, and this site is one more pair of hands.*

Almost every game, exercise and idea on this site is **inspired by, derived from, or descended from** the work of the teachers, authors, theatres and communities listed below. We did not invent this art form. We are students of it, and we are grateful to every person on this page.

If this site gives you anything at all, please pass the thanks upstream: **[train with these organizations](#theatres-organizations)**, and **[buy these authors' books](#books)**. That is the only payment any of this deserves.

!!! warning "Rights, credit and what this project is"
    **The respective authors, teachers and organizations retain all rights to their intellectual property** — their techniques, their curricula, their written work, their brands and their trademarks. Nothing on this site transfers, dilutes or claims any of it.

    **Zen Sandbox is a not-for-profit endeavour.** There are no course fees, no advertising and nothing for sale. It exists so that people who cannot reach a physical theatre can still practise the art form.

    **On game rules and our own writing.** The *rules* of a game are a method of play and are not protected by copyright; the *specific written expression* of them is. Every activity description on this site is **our own re-authoring**, written from scratch in our own words. Where an activity descends from a published game we say so on the card, in its **Lineage** section, naming the tradition and the source.

    **On trademarks.** *ComedySportz®* and *CSz®* are registered marks of ComedySportz / CSz Worldwide. *Theatresports™* is associated with Keith Johnstone and the International Theatresports Institute. *The Second City*, *Upright Citizens Brigade*, *iO*, *The Annoyance* and *The Groundlings* are the marks of their respective owners. They are used here only as attributive references to a tradition or a lineage. **No affiliation, licence, sponsorship or endorsement is implied.**

    If you own rights to anything referenced here and would like a credit corrected, a description changed, or a page removed — **please tell us and we will do it.**

## 📚 Books

**We own more than 16 of the books below, and we would wholeheartedly encourage you to buy the originals.**

Improv is not a multi-million-dollar business. These books were written from inside small theatres and rehearsal rooms by people who were teaching all week and writing at night, and many of them are still teaching today. There is no large industry behind them. **Every single copy sold matters to the author**, and every contribution goes back to the person who worked this craft out and then chose to write it down rather than keep it.

So please: buy them, read them slowly, and let them do to you what they did to us. No summary — ours included — comes close to sitting with the original. This is how you fall in love with the art form.

## 🎓 Thought Leaders & Teachers

The people whose thinking this framework is built on — whether through their books, their teaching, their theatres, or the students they sent out into the world.

**The foundations** — Viola Spolin, who invented theater games and gave the form its pedagogy · Keith Johnstone, who gave us status, spontaneity, storytelling and Theatresports · Del Close, who gave us the Harold and long-form itself.

**Carrying it forward** — Charna Halpern · Mick Napier · Kim "Howard" Johnson · Matt Besser, Ian Roberts & Matt Walsh · Patricia Ryan Madson · Tom Salinsky & Deborah Frances-White · Katie Goodman · James Thomas Bailey · Rob Russell · Will Hines · Jill Bernard · Kevin Mullaney · Jeffrey Sweet · Stephen Nachmanovitch · Amy Poehler · Tina Fey.

And the thousands of unnamed teachers, coaches and players who kept these games alive by playing them.

## 🏛️ Theatres & Organizations

The institutions that built, taught and protected this art form.

**We would highly recommend training with these reputed organizations. They have exceptional, deeply experienced trainers** — people who have spent decades in the room, and who can see and correct things in you that no online curriculum ever will. If you can get to one of them, in person or online, go. We say the same thing on our [invitation page](pilot-class-invitation.md): this community is a supplement to that training, never a substitute for it.

| Organization | Known for |
|---|---|
| [The Second City](https://www.secondcity.com/) | The original improv-to-sketch pipeline; Spolin's methods in practice |
| [ComedySportz / CSz Worldwide](https://cszworldwide.com/) | Competitive short-form; the match format and its rulebook |
| [Upright Citizens Brigade](https://ucbcomedy.com/) | The UCB Harold, and long-form teaching at scale |
| iO Theater (ImprovOlympic) | Del Close's home; the Harold's birthplace |
| The Annoyance Theatre | Napier's school; character-first, "do something" improv |
| The Groundlings | Character and sketch craft |
| Loose Moose Theatre | Johnstone's company; where Theatresports began |
| International Theatresports Institute | Stewards of the Theatresports™ tradition |
| BATS Improv · Magnet Theater · [Hoopla Impro](https://hooplaimpro.com/) | Long-form and short-form teaching communities |

## 💛 And finally

To everyone who has ever said **"Yes, And"** to a bad idea and made it a good one — thank you. This whole tradition is built out of that one act of generosity, repeated for seventy years.

To the teachers who kept small theatres alive on thin margins, to the authors who wrote it all down instead of keeping it, and to the players who handed these games to the next person in the circle: **we are standing entirely on your work, and we know it.**

The best thanks any of us can offer is the obvious one. **Take their classes. Buy their books.** Then go and play.
