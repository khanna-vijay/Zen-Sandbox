# Balloon Escape

![Balloon Escape — infographic](img/LB__balloon-escape.jpg){ .infographic }

> Everyone inflates like a party balloon, then lets go and flaps round the room on a long raspberry until they run out of air.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 1/5` · `Energy high` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

A room that arrived holding its breath ends up loud, floppy and out of air together, with nothing left to protect.

**Childhood borrow:** Letting go of an untied balloon and watching it fart across the room.

## Setup

Push chairs and bags to the walls so there is clear floor. Everyone stands scattered, arm's length apart, facing no particular direction. No props.

## How to run it

1. Frame it in one line: we are party balloons, we get blown up, then someone lets go. Demonstrate once yourself, full volume, full flapping. 45 seconds.
2. Round one, small balloon. All together: three sipping breaths in, arms and cheeks swelling, then release on a raspberry and wobble on the spot until empty. Twice. 45 seconds.
3. Round two, bigger balloon. Six breaths in, rise onto toes, then release and travel — eyes up, weaving through the gaps, raspberry until the air is gone. Twice. 60 seconds.
4. Round three, enormous balloon. Ten breaths, arms wide, everyone hissing at full stretch. On your count, release: longest raspberry, biggest flapping, finish crumpled on the floor. 75 seconds.
5. Finale, the slow leak. Everyone on the floor lets out one thin squeaking whine together, getting quieter, until the room is flat and silent. 45 seconds.
6. Ask the one debrief question from the floor. 30 seconds, then straight on.

## Side-coach

- *"More air! You are not full yet!"*
- *"Let go — flap, don't run!"*
- *"Eyes up, find the gaps!"*
- *"Every last bit of air out!"*
- *"Flatter. Flatter. Gone."*

## Getting past the cringe

Do the full demo before you explain anything — inflate, release, flap across the floor, land in a heap. Get up, say nothing about it, and start the count. Your commitment is the permission; do not ask for theirs.

## Opt-down

Stay standing and inflate with the breath only — no raspberry, just a long hiss on the way out, hands sinking to your sides. Same timing, same room, quarter of the volume.optional; kneeling or bending at the waist replaces the floor collapse.

## If it stalls

- The breaths in are too polite. Make the inflation audible and greedy — hiss the intake yourself so they hear it.
- If nobody travels, shrink the room: call everyone into a tight cluster so releasing means bumping past each other and the wobble does the work.

## Ask afterwards

1. Shout out one word for how your body feels right now.

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One shared space, everyone travels on rounds two and three. Run round two twice as written. |
| 14–17 | Same structure, but call two travel directions before round two — odd side of the room goes clockwise, even side anticlockwise — so the weaving has a shape and collisions drop. |
| 18–20 | No travelling. All three rounds are released on the spot, spinning and wobbling within one arm's length. Add a third release to round two to keep the volume up. |
