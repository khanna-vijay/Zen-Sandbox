# Let Go of the Balloon

![Let Go of the Balloon — infographic](img/LB__let-go-of-the-balloon.jpg){ .infographic }

> The whole room inflates, then flies around the space on a long raspberry and crumples to the floor.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 1/5` · `Energy high` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

Adults who have spent the week keeping their noise down make an undignified sound in unison and end up breathless and grinning, with nothing to get right.

**Childhood borrow:** Blowing up a balloon and letting it go to fart around the room.

## Setup

Clear the floor. Everyone stands scattered, an arm's length from anyone else. No chairs in the walking area.

## How to run it

1. (30s) Demonstrate yourself, full size. Suck in three big breaths, puff your cheeks, pinch your lips, then release a long wet raspberry and stagger round the room until you crumple.
2. (45s) Round one, all together. Count three breaths in. On your 'let go', everyone releases the raspberry, wobbles wherever it takes them, and drops to a squat or the floor when the air runs out.
3. (45s) Round two, squeaky. Same inflate, but the escaping air is a high thin whine. Tell them to go higher than is dignified. You go highest.
4. (60s) Round three, slow puncture. One tiny 'pfft' every couple of seconds, sinking a little lower each time, until everyone is a puddle on the floor. Take the full twenty seconds.
5. (60s) Round four, one giant balloon. Everyone inhales together on your count for five seconds, holds, then releases one enormous shared raspberry and collapses. Do it twice.
6. (30s) Everyone up. Shake hands and feet out. Three loud exhales together to reset breathing.
7. (30s) One question, answers shouted out, then move straight on.

## Side-coach

- *"Bigger breath — fill the whole balloon."*
- *"Let the noise be ugly."*
- *"Follow where the air throws you, don't steer."*
- *"Higher and squeakier than that."*
- *"All the air out. Nothing left."*

## Getting past the cringe

Do the first one alone, at full size, before you explain anything — wet raspberry, staggering, the lot. Say 'that's the whole game' and count them straight into round one. Do not wait for buy-in and do not comment on anyone's volume but your own.

## Opt-down

Stay standing and still, hands miming a shrinking balloon, and let the air out as a quiet hiss instead of a raspberry. Same rounds, same counts, same room.

## If it stalls

- Drop the travelling and keep everyone on the spot — the noise carries the round on its own.
- Do a round where you are the only balloon and they all count you in. Watching you go fully ridiculous once usually opens the room.

## Ask afterwards

1. Shout one word for how your body feels now.

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One open floor, all four rounds, travelling allowed on rounds one and two. |
| 14–17 | One open floor but keep travelling to three steps maximum. Run the giant balloon in round four twice as written. |
| 18–20 | Split into two groups at opposite ends of the room. Everyone stays on the spot, no travelling. Cut round three to keep to five minutes; groups still inflate and release simultaneously. |

## Safety & inclusion

No contact. Tell people to keep eyes up while wobbling and to walk, never run. Crumpling to the floor is optional — a squat or a soft bend at the knees does the same job. Anyone light-headed from the big breaths should stop inflating and just make the sound; say this before round one.

## Lineage

In the Laughter yoga breathing games and children's drama warm-ups tradition. Laughter yoga uses deep inhale-and-release breathing to trigger involuntary laughing; children's drama classes use the balloon image for body awareness. Combined and rebuilt here purely as a noise-making blast, with a collective giant balloon at the end so nobody is ever the only one making the sound.
