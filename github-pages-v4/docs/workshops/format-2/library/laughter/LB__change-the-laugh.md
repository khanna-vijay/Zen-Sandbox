# Change the Laugh

![Change the Laugh — infographic](img/LB__change-the-laugh.jpg){ .infographic }

> Everyone laughs a fake laugh at the same time, and you keep swapping the style until it stops being fake.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 1/5` · `Energy high` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

A room of strangers goes from arms-folded and polite to loud, breathless and pink-faced in five minutes. The fake laughing turns real somewhere around the belly laugh, and after that nobody is protecting their dignity.

**Childhood borrow:** Fake-laughing on purpose with other kids until it collapses into the real thing you cannot stop.

## Setup

One circle, everyone standing, an arm's length between shoulders. Push bags and chairs to the walls so there is a clear floor to walk on. No props.

## How to run it

1. (0:00-0:30) Stand in the circle with them. Say: we are all going to fake-laugh together, all at once, nobody watching anybody. It is meant to sound terrible. You laugh first and loudest.
2. (0:30-1:00) Call 'tiny mouse laugh'. Everyone squeaks a high, thin laugh through closed teeth for thirty seconds. Keep yours going the whole time.
3. (1:00-1:40) Call 'belly laugh'. Hands on your own stomach, big open 'HA HA HA' from low down. Forty seconds. Get louder yourself when the room dips.
4. (1:40-2:10) Call 'silent laugh'. Mouths wide open, shoulders shaking, no sound at all. Thirty seconds. Look at the ceiling, not at each other.
5. (2:10-2:50) Call 'slow villain laugh'. Start low and quiet, build to enormous over forty seconds. Rub your hands together. Everyone builds together.
6. (2:50-3:40) Call 'wobbly walk'. Everyone laughs and wanders the room on soft, floppy knees for fifty seconds. Eyes up, no touching, keep laughing while walking.
7. (3:40-4:30) Call everyone back to the circle. Fifty seconds of 'your own worst laugh, full volume', then raise a hand for instant silence and three slow breaths together.
8. (4:30-5:00) Ask the one debrief question. Take three or four short answers, then move straight on.

## Side-coach

- *"Fake is fine. Keep it going."*
- *"Louder than feels sensible."*
- *"From the belly, not the throat."*
- *"Eyes up, keep walking."*
- *"Nobody is watching you."*

## Getting past the cringe

You laugh first, loudest and ugliest, and you do not stop when the first thirty seconds are thin. Name it out loud while still laughing: "This is completely fake and that is the point." Never single anyone out or ask why they are not joining in — the room copies your commitment, not your instruction.

## Opt-down

Stay in the circle and do the whole thing on a breathy whisper with a hand on your own belly. For the walking step, sway on the spot instead of moving. Everyone is still doing it together; nobody is standing at the side.

## If it stalls

- Drop to the silent laugh — no sound means no self-consciousness, and it usually breaks people first.
- Go louder yourself and name what is happening: 'That's the awkward bit. Stay in it, it passes.'
- Shorten the remaining flavours to twenty seconds each so the switches come fast and nobody has time to think.

## Ask afterwards

1. One word for how the room feels now — go round, fast.

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One circle. Run all five flavours as written. |
| 14–17 | One wider circle. Everyone turns to face outward for the silent laugh so nobody is looking in at anybody. |
| 18–20 | Two circles at opposite ends of the room. Stand between them and call the flavours for both. Cut the villain laugh so the timing still lands at five minutes. |

## Safety & inclusion

Sustained loud laughing strains voices and can make people light-headed. Tell them to laugh from the belly, not the throat, and to drop to quiet or stop and breathe whenever they want. Anyone with asthma, a chest or rib injury, pregnancy, or recent surgery does the quiet version. During the walking step: eyes up, no contact, and slow the pace if the floor is tight.

## Lineage

In the Laughter yoga / laughter club practice tradition. Laughter clubs use sustained deliberate laughing to trigger the real thing. Rules written from scratch. Changed for a first-night improv room: the breathing and chanting are cut, the laugh style switches every thirty to fifty seconds so nobody settles into embarrassment, and one walking step is added because strangers stiffen when they stand still.
