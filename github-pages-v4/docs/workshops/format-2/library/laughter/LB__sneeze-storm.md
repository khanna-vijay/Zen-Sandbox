# Sneeze Storm

![Sneeze Storm — infographic](img/LB__sneeze-storm.jpg){ .infographic }

> The whole room builds and releases enormous fake sneezes together until the room is one big contagious mess of noise.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 1/5` · `Energy high` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

Convulsive breath and a loud shared noise, done by everyone at once, which drops the shoulders and puts the room in a body rather than a head.

**Childhood borrow:** Faking a giant sneeze to make your friends laugh, and the way one fake sneeze sets off every kid in the room.

## Setup

Loose circle, arm's length apart, chairs pushed back. No props. Everyone stands (or sits — a sneeze works fine seated).

## How to run it

1. (30s) Stand in the circle. Say nothing. Do the biggest, ugliest fake sneeze of your life. Do a second one, worse. Then say: 'Right. Everybody.'
2. (45s) Teach the build: two rising 'ah… ah…' breaths, then the sneeze on your nod. Whole room together, three times. You go loudest every time.
3. (60s) Slow motion. Inhale for a slow count of eight, face screwing up, then release the sneeze over five seconds, folding at the waist. Twice.
4. (60s) Contagion round. You sneeze; anyone it 'hits' sneezes; keep passing it until the whole circle is sneezing at once. Let it run messy for twenty seconds.
5. (45s) Rapid fire. Ten tiny mouse sneezes, then ten enormous ones, everybody simultaneously, no counting, no order.
6. (30s) Call freeze. One shared slow inhale together. Shake out arms and legs.
7. (30s) One question, quick answers, then move straight on.

## Side-coach

- *"Bigger than you think. Nobody is watching you."*
- *"Let the whole body go with it — knees, shoulders, face."*
- *"Ugly is correct. Pretty sneezes don't count."*
- *"Catch it! It's going round!"*
- *"Tiny mouse sneezes… now enormous."*

## Getting past the cringe

Do two enormous sneezes before you explain anything. No preamble, no 'this might feel silly'. The room decides how big to go by watching how big you went, so go embarrassingly big, twice, and then look completely unbothered.

## Opt-down

Sneeze at mouse volume with the full face and shoulders, or sit and do the build-up breaths with no sound on the release. You are still inside the circle and still on the same count as everyone else.

## If it stalls

- If people stay polite, drop the volume instead: whisper-tiny sneezes for fifteen seconds, then jump to full size. The contrast breaks the reserve.
- If the contagion round dies, call names — 'it's got Sam, it's got the whole left side' — and keep sneezing yourself until it spreads.

## Ask afterwards

1. Where in your body did that land? One word each, go.

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One circle. Run the contagion round twice. |
| 14–17 | One circle, slightly wider. Run the contagion round once but start it from two people on opposite sides. |
| 18–20 | Two circles of nine or ten, one facilitator-led, one led by whoever sneezed hardest in step two. Both run at the same time. Nobody watches. |

## Safety & inclusion

Keep the head-forward motion gentle — bend from the waist, not a neck whip. Anyone with neck, back or voice concerns does the arms and face only, no fold. It is all mimed: nothing actually leaves anyone's mouth, and sneezing into your own elbow is encouraged. Don't force the voice; volume comes from the belly, not the throat.

## Lineage

In the Laughter yoga and clown warm-up practice tradition. Laughter practitioners use fake sneezing as a contagion trigger because the body cannot tell a performed convulsion from a real one. Clown warm-ups use the same shape for release. Rules written from scratch here, timed to five minutes and staged tiny-to-enormous so nobody has to be the loudest person first.
