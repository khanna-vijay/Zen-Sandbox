# The Big Sneeze

![The Big Sneeze — infographic](img/LB__the-big-sneeze.jpg){ .infographic }

> The whole room builds one enormous ridiculous sneeze together, over and over, until the sneezes turn into real laughter.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 1/5` · `Energy high` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

A roomful of adults making an enormous involuntary-sounding noise at the same instant, so that the first real laugh arrives without anyone having tried to cause it.

**Childhood borrow:** Fake-sneezing at the dinner table to crack up your siblings.

## Setup

Clear the floor. Everyone scattered, arm's length apart, facing no particular direction. No chairs, no props.

## How to run it

1. (0:30) Scatter the room. Demonstrate first and biggest: build 'aaah… aaah…' for three seconds, then blow an enormous sneeze into your elbow, whole body folding forward.
2. (1:00) All together now. You call the build, everyone inhales and 'aaah's, you call 'AND—', everyone sneezes at once. Any sound they like. Three times.
3. (1:00) Same, but the sneeze pushes them backwards two steps. Feet stay under them. Call the build, call the release. Four rounds, faster each time.
4. (1:00) Size roulette. Call 'mouse' — tiny squeaked sneeze. Call 'elephant' — vast one that drops them onto one knee. Alternate unpredictably. Six or seven calls.
5. (1:00) Final chain: build for a full eight seconds while everyone circles their arms, then release the biggest one. Let the laughing run. Do it twice.
6. (0:30) One question, then move straight on.

## Side-coach

- *"Into your elbow — no spraying the neighbours."*
- *"Louder than feels sensible. I'm doing it too."*
- *"Let the sneeze move your whole body, not just your face."*
- *"Nobody is watching you. Everyone's mid-sneeze."*
- *"Mouse! Now elephant!"*

## Getting past the cringe

Do the first sneeze before you finish explaining it — mid-sentence, full volume, body folded in half. Then carry straight on talking as if nothing happened. Never ask if they're ready and never comment on how silly it is.

## Opt-down

Stay standing with everyone, do the build and the 'aaah' at whatever volume suits, and skip the body drop entirely — feet planted, sound only.

## If it stalls

- Go bigger yourself and stop watching them. If your sneeze is the loudest in the room, theirs will follow within two rounds.
- Add the failed sneeze: build, build, build — and nothing comes. Then a monstrous one on the next breath. The anticipation does the work.

## Ask afterwards

1. What did your face do that it hasn't done all week?

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One scatter, all together. Run four rounds in step 3 and seven calls in step 4. |
| 14–17 | One scatter, but spread wider so nobody sneezes into a shoulder. Same round counts. Project your calls over the noise. |
| 18–20 | Split into two halves at opposite ends of the room. They sneeze alternately at each other — one half builds and releases, then the other. Three exchanges per step. Everybody is still sneezing; nobody sits out. |

## Safety & inclusion

Sneeze into the elbow, not at each other. Warn against snapping the head or neck — the movement is the torso folding, not a whip. Anyone with neck, back, spinal or pregnancy concerns does the sound only, feet planted, no body drop. Keep arm's length spacing so backward steps meet nobody.

## Lineage

In the Laughter yoga and clown warm-ups tradition. Laughter yoga uses sustained deliberate laughter until it turns genuine; clown training uses the delayed, oversized sneeze as a physical gag. We fused the two, made it fully simultaneous so no one is observed, added the mouse/elephant size call to keep it unpredictable, and cut every element that could be done well or badly.
