# Stand By It

![Stand By It — infographic](img/IB__stand-by-it.jpg){ .infographic }

> People place themselves on an invisible line between two real habits, then tell the neighbour beside them why they landed there.

`Players 10–20` · `~5 min` · `Complexity 2/5` · `Energy medium` · `Contact none` · `Props: none`

## The point

The room sorts itself by real habits in ninety seconds, and people discover who they are standing next to before the show talk starts.

**Everyone has spoken within 75 seconds.**

**What it reveals about the group:** Who commits to an extreme and who negotiates a precise inch of the line · Who explains themselves in one clean sentence and who keeps talking past the cue · Who watches the room before choosing a spot and who moves on instinct · How the group actually feels about playing to friends tonight

## Setup

Push bags and chairs to the walls so there is a clear strip across the room. Point at one wall and the opposite wall — that strip is the line. No props.

## How to run it

1. (30s) Name the line. One wall is one extreme, the far wall the other, everywhere between counts. Tell them the middle is banned: pick a side, however slightly.
2. (60s) Prompt one: 'I cancel plans and feel fine' to 'I have never cancelled anything in my life.' They walk and place themselves. Then 20 seconds each with the nearest person: one true sentence why.
3. (60s) Prompt two: 'I sing in the car at full volume' to 'total silence in the car.' Walk, place, new neighbour, one sentence each.
4. (60s) Prompt three: 'I want people I know in the audience tonight' to 'I'd rather play to complete strangers.' Walk, place, new neighbour, one sentence each.
5. (60s) Final round: from where they stand, each person calls out five words or fewer on that last prompt. Go along the line, one end to the other. No commentary.
6. (30s) Tell them to look at the shape of the line, then break to the circle. No discussion.

## Side-coach

- *"Feet decide. Move."*
- *"No middle. Pick a side, even by a step."*
- *"One sentence each, then stop."*
- *"New neighbour every round."*
- *"Five words or fewer. Next."*

## If it stalls

- Everyone clusters in the centre. Say the middle metre is closed and physically walk through it until they scatter.
- Pairs go quiet after four seconds. Give them the sentence stem: 'I'm standing here because...'
- One person has no neighbour at the end of the line. Send them to the nearest pair to make a three.

## Ask afterwards

1. Where did you stand where you expected to be alone and weren't?
2. Anyone move further than they meant to?

## Scale

| Class size | What changes |
|---|---|
| 10–13 | One line, three prompts as written. The final five-word round takes about 45 seconds. |
| 14–17 | One line still works. Cut the neighbour talk to 15 seconds each and start the final round from the middle outwards so it fits 60 seconds. |
| 18–20 | Run two parallel lines, one down each half of the room, using the same prompts. Do the final five-word round in both lines at once, facilitator standing between them. |

## Safety & inclusion

No contact. Watch for bags and chair legs in the walking strip. Anyone who would rather not move can stay put and show their position on a raised hand from zero to ten; that counts. Any prompt can be passed on without explanation.

## Lineage

In the Continuum or spectrum lines from applied drama and workshop facilitation tradition. The physical opinion line is common facilitation furniture. Added the compulsory one-sentence exchange with whoever you land beside, banned the middle so people commit, and closed with a five-word round so nobody leaves without speaking to the whole room.
