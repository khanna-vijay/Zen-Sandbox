# Sources & Acknowledgements

This curriculum stands on six books and a long oral tradition. **Every activity description on this site is our own re-authoring.** No rules text, examples or prose have been reproduced from any source. Where an activity descends from a published game we say so on the card, in its **Lineage** section, naming the tradition and the book.

## On what is and isn't ours

Game *rules* are methods of play and are not protected by copyright; the *specific written expression* of them is. We re-author every activity from scratch, for a three-hour adult class of 10–20 people, in our own voice and our own schema. Any resemblance in wording to a source is unintended — tell us and we will change it.
