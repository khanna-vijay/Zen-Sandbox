# 🎒 Homework — Week 01 · Re-contract & The Two-Person Scene

> Last session you named the odd thing in a scene out loud; this week you practise finding it faster and playing it on purpose.

## 🧘 Solo drills this week

| Drill | Time | What to notice |
|---|---|---|
| [Scene](../../../solo-practice/24_scene.md) | 10 minutes, five days | Notice the first moment something stops being ordinary. It is usually small: a word said twice, an odd preference, a reaction bigger than the thing that caused it. Mark it. Say the cue to yourself — if this is true, what else is true? Then notice whether your next move builds on that oddity or steps away to something new. Stepping away is the common failure and it feels like progress at the time. Also notice how long you spend on setup before the first unusual thing appears. If it is more than a few lines, note where you could have let it arrive sooner. |

## 🌍 Beyond the stage — Let Your Life Become Effortless

Pick one task this week that you normally push through by force — a difficult email, a tidy-up, a conversation you keep postponing. Before you start it, sit for two minutes and do nothing about it. Then begin with the easiest available part, even if it seems too small to matter. Send one line instead of three paragraphs. Clear one shelf. Ask one question. Stop when the ease runs out rather than when the task is finished, and write down the time you stopped. Do this once a day, five days. At the end of the week, look at your five notes. You have failed this practice if you cannot say which part of each task moved on its own and which part you dragged.

## ✍️ Journal

1. Describe a moment this week when someone did something slightly unusual and everyone carried on as if it were normal. What would have happened if you had named it?
2. Where in your own behaviour is the oddity that other people have noticed and you have not? Write what you think it is, without defending it.
3. When you force something at work or at home, what does the forcing feel like in your body? Write three physical signs you could catch early.

## 👀 Watch / notice

Watch one short scene from any comedy — television, film, a clip online, three to five minutes is enough. Watch it twice. On the second pass, write down the exact moment the first unusual thing appears, and then list every later beat that is a version of that same unusual thing.

**Bring back:** The title of the scene, one sentence naming the unusual thing, and your count of how many times it was repeated or escalated.

## 📌 Bring to next session

- Journal entries from the week
- The watch-and-notice scene note with the repeat count
- Five daily notes from the effortless-task practice
