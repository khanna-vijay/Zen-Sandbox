# 🎒 Homework — Week 02 · A → C — Mining the Suggestion

> Last session you kept landing on the first association and watching the room nod politely; this week's reps are about getting past that first thought on purpose.

## 🧘 Solo drills this week

| Drill | Time | What to notice |
|---|---|---|
| [Dada Monologue](../../../solo-practice/01_dada-monologue.md) | 8-10 minutes | Notice the exact moment your mouth wants to return to the obvious. Take a suggestion word, say the first thing it makes you think of out loud, then keep going and count how many steps you travel before an association arrives that you could not have predicted at the start. Usually it is three or four. Notice whether the useful material comes from a concept (an idea about the word) or a specific (a person, a smell, a Tuesday afternoon). Specifics travel further. Also notice what you censor: if you skipped a step because it felt boring or too personal, say it out loud next time and see where it leads. |

## 🌍 Beyond the stage — Name the Game

Go to one live thing this week. A gig, a comedy night, a church service, an open mic, a school concert, a market busker. Free or cheap is fine. Stay for at least forty minutes. Do not take notes during it. On the way home, write one sentence in your phone that says what the performers were actually doing all night — not what it was called, not whether you enjoyed it. Something like: "Four people took turns being the one who cared least." Or: "He kept promising a story and giving us a list instead." One sentence. If your sentence is a description of the genre rather than the behaviour, you have not found it yet. Rewrite it once. You have failed this only if you leave the week without a sentence.


## ✍️ Journal

1. Think of a subject you get asked about often — your job, your hometown, your family. Write the answer you always give. Then write the third answer down, the one you have never said out loud because it takes too long to explain.
2. Where in your life do you reach for the obvious response because it is faster, and what does that speed cost you?

## 👀 Watch / notice

Pick one word you overhear this week — on a train, in a shop, from a colleague. Follow it privately for two minutes and write down the chain of five things it made you think of, in order.

**Bring back:** The written chain of five, and be ready to say which link on it you would actually want to play a scene about.

## 📌 Bring to next session

- The five-link association chain, written down
- One sentence naming what the live performers were doing all night
- A note of how many association steps it usually took before something surprised you
- Any suggestion word from Week 1 you feel the group under-mined
