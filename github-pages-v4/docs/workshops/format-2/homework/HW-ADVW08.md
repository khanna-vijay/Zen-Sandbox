# 🎒 Homework — Week 08 · The Public Set

> Last session you ran the full set with an audience in the room; this week's reps are about being visible and clear in service of the piece rather than your slot in it.

## 🧘 Solo drills this week

| Drill | Time | What to notice |
|---|---|---|
| [Reaching Out](../../../solo-practice/15_reaching-out.md) | 6-8 minutes | Notice the moment your attention curls back onto yourself — checking how you sounded, wondering if that was good. Mark it. Then notice what changes in your body when you send the reach outward instead: where your weight goes, whether your chest opens, whether your voice lands at the far wall or dies at your feet. Track one more thing: whether reaching out makes you louder, or just clearer. They are not the same. Clarity is doing one thing at a time so it can be seen. Loudness is often panic. At the end of each session, say out loud in one sentence what you were reaching for. If you can't, the reach was decorative. |

## 🌍 Beyond the stage — Doorway In, Doorway Out

Pick one thing this week that matters to you and recurs — a meeting, a phone call home, the school run, a shift. Build a thirty-second routine you do before it, every time. Keep it small and physical: three slow breaths, hands flat on a surface, one sentence to yourself about what you are there to do. Same words each time. Same order. Then build a ten-second routine for afterwards: wash your hands, or step through a specific doorway, or put your phone face down, and say one sentence that ends it. "Done. Not mine now." Do both, six times minimum, across the week. Log a tick each time. You have failed it if you improvised the ritual, or skipped the ending one because the thing went badly. The ending one matters most on those days.

## ✍️ Journal

1. Think of a time you did a small job well and nobody noticed. What did you feel, honestly, in the hour afterwards? What does that tell you about what you want from being on stage?
2. Where in your life do you already serve something bigger than your part in it — a team, a family, a project? Write down what that costs you and what it gives back.
3. Write about the last time you replayed a performance, a conversation or a meeting in your head afterwards. How long did the replay run? What would have stopped it sooner?

## 👀 Watch / notice

Watch any live performance with more than one performer in it — theatre, stand-up on video, a band, a church service, a stand-up meeting at work. Pick one performer who is not the focus and watch only them for five minutes.

**Bring back:** One sentence on a specific thing that performer did to make somebody else's moment land, and one sentence on what it looked like from the outside when they stopped supporting and started competing.

## 📌 Bring to next session

- Your written sentence naming what you were reaching for in the Reaching Out drill, from your worst day of the week.
- Your tick log from Doorway In, Doorway Out, including the times you skipped it.
- The two sentences from the watching task.
- One line: the thing you most want the audience to get from the set, stated without reference to yourself.
